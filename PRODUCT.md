# Floorplan: product one-pager

**What it is:** Drag people into teams, rooms, and shared spaces. A team map builder: groups, sub-groups, shared bands, percentage splits, read as a nested-box diagram or as a pixel office, written as reusable YAML.

**Who it is for:** An engineering manager or staff engineer planning a reorg or explaining the current one. They have a dozen people, three or four teams, a shared QA or on-call pool, and two people who are split. They need a picture they can change in a minute and a text file they can diff.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** A laptop on a desk, an hour before a planning call, with a spreadsheet of names open in another tab. Dark room, dark theme. Dense: one screen should hold twenty people and five groups without scrolling.

**What done looks like:** A map where every person sums to 100%, every room is named, and the YAML is saved or shared as a link, ready to paste into the planning doc.
